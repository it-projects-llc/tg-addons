===========================================
 Signup modifications for Tribal Gathering
===========================================

* Increases font of "Reset password" and "Signup buttons" under "Login" button
  and wraps them in buttons for better visibility

* Signup URL depends on current website

* 'Password reset' email is sent from website's company email

* Saves redirect param when registering using auth_signup_verify_email
